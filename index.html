Features

Everything you need to follow a flight

From a quick look at who is flying over your house to planning around turbulence, all in one calm, fast window.

Track every flight
Flight card

All about a flight, on one card

Click any plane. The card shows who flies it, where it is going and how it is doing right now.

  • Airline, aircraft type, registration and photo, with the flag of the country it is registered in
  • Route with an animated flight line, distance left and estimated arrival time
  • Altitude, speed, vertical speed, and head or tail wind
  • Today's flights of the same aircraft, drawn on the map with one click
  • An altitude and speed chart, and a button that saves a picture of the flight to share

Live flights

Positions refresh every 15 seconds. Move the map and the new area loads right away.

Range filters

Altitude and speed ranges with two handles each, plus airport, airline and aircraft type.

Replay

Rewind the last hour. A label above the slider shows exactly which time you are viewing.

Watchlist

Watch a plane and get a notification when it takes off or lands, anywhere in the world.

Alert zone

Draw a circle on the map and get notified when an aircraft enters it.

Emergency alerts

Aircraft squawking 7500, 7600 or 7700 get a blinking red ring and a notification.

Weather & safety
Turbulence ahead

Know about turbulence before it happens

SkyTrack looks at the route ahead of the selected aircraft, at its altitude, and tells you in plain words.

  • Green: nothing reported or forecast ahead
  • Yellow: moderate turbulence possible
  • Red: high risk of turbulence
  • A map layer draws official SIGMET and G-AIRMET turbulence areas worldwide

Based on advisories and recent pilot reports. Green means nothing is reported, not a guarantee.

Wind & fuel

See the wind your flight is flying in

Turn on wind arrows at any flight level and see jet streams as they form. On the card, SkyTrack shows head or tail wind and a rough fuel and CO₂ estimate.

  • Wind arrows colored by speed, at six flight levels from FL050 to FL390
  • Estimated fuel and CO₂ for the rest of the flight, with a bar for the fuel left
  • Estimated arrival time based on the current ground speed
Airport panel

Click an airport, get the whole picture

Current weather, the raw METAR and TAF with its VFR/IFR category, and live arrivals and departures. Click a flight in the list to open it.

  • Temperature, wind, gusts, visibility and pressure
  • Arrivals and departures with status (approaching, landing), distance and time
  • City and country, and a rain radar layer on the map

Pick your spot on the map or type an airport code. SkyTrack lists the aircraft around you and tells you where to look.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is SkyTrack really free?

Yes. It is free and open source under the MIT license. There are no ads, no account and no API key.

Why does Windows warn me when I run the installer?

The installer is not code-signed yet, and Windows warns about every unsigned app. Choose “More info”, then “Run anyway”. The full source code is on GitHub if you want to check it or build it yourself.

Does it work on macOS and Linux?

Yes. Installers for Windows, macOS (.dmg) and Linux (.AppImage) are built with every release, and the download button picks the right one for your computer. macOS may warn because the app is not signed yet: right-click the app and choose Open.

Where does the data come from?

Open community data: adsb.lol and adsb.fi for live positions, OpenSky as a backup, adsbdb.com for routes and photos, Open-Meteo and aviationweather.gov for weather, wind and turbulence. See the list below.

I only see a few planes. Why?

The free feeds have strict rate limits and only cover part of a wide view. Zoom in and more aircraft appear. Coverage is thinner over oceans and some countries.

What does a green turbulence line mean?

It means no turbulence is reported or forecast on the route ahead, at that altitude. It is not a guarantee: clear-air turbulence often cannot be predicted.

How accurate are the fuel and CO₂ numbers?

They are rough estimates from typical cruise fuel burn per aircraft type. The real fuel load of a flight is not public, so treat the bar as an illustration.

How do I update SkyTrack?

From version 0.7.0 the Windows and Linux apps update themselves: a bar appears at the bottom of the menu when a new version is ready. On macOS the bar links to the download. Older versions need one manual update to 0.7.0 first.

Is there a web version?

There is a web demo with simulated flights and turbulence areas, so you can try SkyTrack without installing anything. Live data needs the desktop app, because the free data sources do not allow requests from web pages.

Can I help or report a bug?

Yes, please. Open an issue or a pull request on GitHub.
